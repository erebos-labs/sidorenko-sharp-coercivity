# License scope

Erebos Labs original contributions use the top-level MIT license. This
includes the repository verification wrapper and CI configuration.

The reviewed mathematical package retains its own exact licensing and
attribution files. The three copied OpenAI files in `package/sources/openai/`
retain **Apache-2.0** licensing. The top-level MIT license does not relicense
those third-party copies or remove their obligations.

Read [package/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](package/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md),
[package/sources/openai/LICENSE](package/sources/openai/LICENSE), and
[package/ATTRIBUTION.md](package/ATTRIBUTION.md) for complete scope and credit.
The related theorem and API snapshots are not redistributed.

The variance package in variance/ adds a second original proof/certificate
and clearly scoped MIT contributions. Its three exact OpenAI sections and
license retain Apache-2.0. Its prior Erebos theorem and checker adaptation
retain the restored MIT notice in variance/sources/prior/LICENSE.
See variance/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md and variance/ATTRIBUTION.md.

The 18 unclear-license refinement sources are citation-only in the public
tree and release ZIP. Their exact repository/commit/path/hash metadata is
retained. Optional local retrieval does not grant redistribution permission;
neither the external source cache nor raw API/PR snapshots are released.

The full-slack package in full-slack/ uses the exact top-level MIT license
for its new original proof, prose, programs and packaging tests. Its copied
published theorem/certificate retains the unaltered MIT source notice; its
three copied OpenAI sections retain Apache-2.0. See
full-slack/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md and the unchanged licenses under
full-slack/sources/. The standalone new-package replay is offline; no
unclear-license external source cache is distributed with it.
