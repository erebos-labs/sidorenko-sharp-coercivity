## Mathematical checks versus finite evidence

The proof covers all activity/sign patterns through the inherited uniform
interface, and all type maps through an algebraic identity and dominance
argument. Finite collapsed-map controls test the implementation; they do not
enumerate or prove the universal map statement by sampling. The same distinction
applies to the small cloned-kernel and H-to-K2 checks versus the general
pushforward and collision proof.

The complete source-derived gain definition and all integer mode premises are
retained. Actual matrix/Lagrangian configurations are counted by the inherited
analytic argument. No claim that arbitrary relaxed rank tuples are realizable
is needed for this transfer, and no relaxed equality tuple is promoted to a
geometric host ingredient.

## Execution boundary

Actual results and hashes are in logs/execution_receipt.json and the individual
stdout/stderr files. The first four-suite orchestration was externally interrupted
after three suites had completed. The inherited mutation suite's successful retry
is separately recorded; no success is inferred from the interrupted attempt.
The final strict archive replay is a separate check after all files are frozen.

No missing mathematical step was identified in the transfer after the stated
rank-layer input. This is not a certification of every assertion in an external
repository, an independent human review, a global priority claim, or a Lean proof.
